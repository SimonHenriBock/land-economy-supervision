1.)	Set the question: “What determines where green industry locates in Europe – do new projects and plants go to high market-potential regions or to regions with high renewable energy supply?”
2.)	Sub-questions: 
	Does the answer differ by sector (i.e. do energy-intensiveness of the product and transport costs matter)?
	Do local price signals matter (countries with national vs zonal electricity pricing)?
3.)	Set hypotheses: 
	H1: market potential dominates (NEG, home market effect)
	H2: energy pull (first-nature advantage, locate near the input that’s costliest to transport)
	H3: path dependence (cumulative causation, brownfield advantages)
4.)	Scope: European NUTS2 regions (~270 regions, 2 project case studies)
